Quick tips

• Drag the pill anywhere · double-click the time for full screen · right-click for the menu
• Click the task line on the pill to set what you’re working on; stats group sessions by task
• Scroll over the pill to change opacity
• In full screen: Space start/pause · S skip · R reset · T task · ←→ theme · Esc exit
• Lost the pill? Click the tray icon, or press the Show/Hide hotkey
